# Dinner AI v0.9.1 Private Beta

## Tester access
Beta code: `DINNER91`

## What this test is for
Please use Dinner AI naturally for about 7–14 days. We care more about where the app is confusing, slow, inaccurate, or broken than whether you use every feature.

## Suggested first test
1. Install Dinner AI.
2. Complete the short onboarding.
3. Enter the beta code.
4. Add several ingredients manually or scan your fridge.
5. Review the detected ingredients and correct anything that is wrong.
6. Open a recommended recipe.
7. Change the serving size.
8. Add missing ingredients to the shopping list.
9. Start Cook Mode and use at least one timer.
10. Finish the recipe and rate it.
11. Close and reopen Dinner AI and make sure your Kitchen, favorites, shopping list, and history are still there.

## Features worth trying

- Serving-size adjustments

## Send feedback whenever something feels wrong
Use the floating **Feedback** button in the app.

Choose:
- Something broke
- Recipe issue
- Suggestion
- I’m confused

Please describe what you expected and what happened. The app automatically includes the beta version and screen name.

## Important beta notes
Dinner AI is still experimental. AI fridge detection and AI recipes can be wrong. Do not rely on Dinner AI as a medical allergy-safety service. Verify ingredient labels, cross-contact risks, food temperatures, and food-safety decisions yourself.

Fridge photos are sent to the Dinner AI backend and an AI service when you choose to scan them. Routine beta analytics do not include the fridge image or your pantry contents.

## Beta usage limits
To protect the test service, each beta session is limited to approximately:
- 12 AI fridge scans per day
- 25 AI-generated recipes per day

Normal recipe browsing, Cook Mode, Kitchen management, and shopping lists do not use those AI limits.
